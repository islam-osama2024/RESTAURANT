About Saveur

Saveur is a French kitchen concept built around one simple idea: good food, carefully made, brings people together.

The idea: plates that would start with fresh ingredients and classic French technique, and end the way a meal should, shared around a table.

Saveur is a demo brand created for this front-end project.
